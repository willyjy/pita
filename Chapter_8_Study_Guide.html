Chapter 8: Takaful (Islamic Insurance)

Exam Preparation Guide with Question Mapping

Exam Weight: 8-10% | Study Time: 5-6 hours

🎯 Key Concepts

Three Forbidden Elements in Conventional Insurance

Why Conventional Insurance is Haram (Forbidden): 1. Al-Gharar (Uncertainty/Ambiguity) - Q1 2. Al-Maisir (Gambling/Speculation) - Q2
3. Al-Riba (Interest/Usury) - Q3

Memory: GMR = Gharar, Maisir, Riba

Sources of Islamic Law

  1. Al-Quran (Word of Allah) - Highest authority
  2. Al-Sunnah (Prophet’s practices)
  3. Ijma (Scholarly consensus)
  4. Qiyas (Analogical reasoning)

Takaful Etymology - Q6

Origin: Kafala = To guarantee, safeguard, preserve
Answer: C (Kafala)

Two Takaful Operating Models - Q7

1. AL-MUDHARABAH (Profit-Sharing Model) - Q8, Q9 - Operator Role: Mudarib (Entrepreneur) - Participant Role: Capital providers
- Profits: SHARED per agreed ratio (e.g., 70:30) - Losses: PARTICIPANTS bear (unless operator negligent)

2. WAKALAH (Agency Model) - Q10 - Operator Role: Wakeel (Agent) - Earnings: Fixed Wakalah fee (not profit share) - Profits: ALL to participants - Losses: Participants bear

Memory: MW Models = Mudharabah + Wakalah

Terminology Comparison

Conventional Takaful
Insured/Policyholder Participant
Premium Contribution
Risk Premium Tabarru (donation)

Concept of Tabarru (Donation) - Q4

Definition: Part of contribution given as donation to help fellow participants

How it works:

Contribution Split:
- Tabarru portion → Mutual pool (for claims)
- Savings portion → Personal account

Purpose: Eliminates gharar/maisir through mutual assistance

Shariah Transaction Requirements - Q5

Three Conditions for Valid Transaction (ALL required): 1. Deliverable - Subject can be delivered 2. Identified - Type and quantity specified 3. Date determined - Delivery timeline clear

Memory: DID = Deliverable, Identified, Date

Answer: D (All the above)

Family vs General Takaful

Family Takaful: - Similar to life insurance (Shariah-compliant) - Long-term savings + protection - Whole life, endowment, investment-linked

General Takaful: - Similar to general insurance (Shariah-compliant) - Short-duration contracts - Q4 - Fire, Motor, Accident, Marine, Engineering

🔑 Quick Reference

FORBIDDEN (GMR):
G = Gharar (Uncertainty)
M = Maisir (Gambling)  
R = Riba (Interest)

MODELS (MW):
M = Mudharabah (Mudarib, Profit-sharing)
W = Wakalah (Wakeel, Fee-based)

TRANSACTION (DID):
D = Deliverable
I = Identified
D = Date determined

KEY TERMS:
Participant (not insured)
Contribution (not premium)
Tabarru (donation - unique to Takaful)
Kafala (origin - guarantee)

⚠️ Common Traps

Trap Wrong Right
Ijma/Akhlak Takaful models Sources/pillars (NOT models)
Guaranteed profits Mudharabah guarantees NO guarantees in Islamic finance
Operator bears loss Both share losses PARTICIPANTS bear (unless negligence)
Wakalah = profit share Wakeel shares profits Wakeel gets FEE only
Two conditions Only i and ii ALL THREE (DID) required

Answers: 1-A, 2-B, 3-D, 4-C, 5-D, 6-C, 7-A, 8-B, 9-C, 10-A
