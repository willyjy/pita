🔥 TOP 50 MUST-KNOW FACTS (By Chapter)

Chapter 1: Understanding Risks (8-10%)

  1. Pure Risk = Loss or no loss (INSURABLE)
  2. Speculative Risk = Profit, loss, or no loss (NOT insurable)
  3. Peril = Cause of loss (fire, theft, death)
  4. Hazard = Condition increasing probability/severity
  5. 3 Hazard Types: Physical (tangible), Moral (dishonesty), Morale (carelessness)

Chapter 2: Risk Management (12-15%)

  1. 4 Risk Techniques (ARRT): Avoidance, Retention, Reduction, Transfer
  2. Insurance = TRANSFER technique
  3. Law of Large Numbers = More exposures → More predictable losses
  4. Pooling = Spread losses among many → Reduce individual impact
  5. Risk Management Process: Identify → Measure → Analyze → Control → Communicate

Chapter 3: Needs Analysis (6-8%)

  1. HLV Formula = (Annual Income × Years to Retirement) - Funeral Costs
  2. Capital Needs = Debts + Education + Emergency + Goals
  3. Income Needs = Dependency Period × Annual Income Need
  4. Emergency Fund = 6-12 months expenses
  5. Dependency Period = Years until youngest child independent

Chapter 4: Life Insurance (15-18%) - HIGHEST WEIGHT

  1. 3 Basic Types: TERM (pure protection), WHOLE LIFE (lifetime), ENDOWMENT (savings+protection)
  2. Term = Lowest premium, no cash value
  3. Whole Life = Level premium, builds cash value, lifetime coverage
  4. Endowment = Highest premium, guaranteed maturity, high cash value
  5. 3 Non-forfeiture Options: CASH, RPT (Reduced Paid-up Term), EPT (Extended Paid-up Term)
  6. 4 Settlement Options: Lump Sum, Installment, Interest Only, Life Income
  7. Grace Period = 30 days (annual) / 7 days (other modes)
  8. Reinstatement = Within 2 years
  9. Incontestability = 2 years (cannot contest after)
  10. Suicide Clause = 1 year (no payout if suicide within)

Chapter 5: Medical Insurance (10-12%)

  1. MRTA = Reducing coverage, level premium (for LOANS)
  2. MLTA = Level coverage, level premium (more protection)
  3. Deductible = You pay first (e.g., first RM500)
  4. Co-insurance = Share % after deductible (e.g., 80/20)
  5. Annual Limit = Maximum per year
  6. Waiting Periods: General (30 days), Pre-existing (120 days)
  7. Medical Card = Room & Board + scheduled benefits
  8. H&S = Indemnity, actual expenses

Chapter 6: Annuities (8-10%)

  1. Annuity = Reverse life insurance (protection against living too long)
  2. 2 Phases: Accumulation (pay in), Liquidation (pay out)
  3. Fixed Annuity = Guaranteed payments, has inflation risk
  4. Variable Annuity = Payments vary with investment, inflation hedge
  5. Pure Life Annuity = NOTHING to heirs (highest payment)
  6. Annuity Certain = Guaranteed period (something to heirs)
  7. Malaysia Tax: RM3,000 deduction (2012-2025), Income exempt (since 1995)

Chapter 7: General Insurance (12-15%)

  1. 3 Principles: INDEMNITY (restore original), CONTRIBUTION (share among insurers), SUBROGATION (recover from third party)
  2. Motor Levels: Act < Third Party < TPFT < Comprehensive
  3. NCD Rates: 25% → 30% → 38.33% → 45% → 55% (max)
  4. RTA 1987 = Motor insurance MANDATORY
  5. Penalty: RM1,000 fine + 3 months jail + 12-month disqualification
  6. 3 Construction Bonds (TAP): Tender (bidding), Advance Payment (upfront $), Performance (completion)
  7. CAR = During INSTALLATION/construction
  8. Machinery Breakdown = During OPERATION (after installed)
  9. Employer’s Liability = Covers EMPLOYER’S fault only (not employee’s negligence)
  10. Workmen’s Comp = For workmen WITHOUT SOCSO

Chapter 8: Takaful (8-10%)

  1. 3 Forbidden (GMR): Gharar (uncertainty), Maisir (gambling), Riba (interest)
  2. Kafala = Root word meaning “to guarantee”
  3. Tabarru = Donation to mutual pool
  4. 2 Models (MW): Mudharabah (profit-sharing), Wakalah (agency)
  5. Mudharabah: Mudarib (entrepreneur), profits SHARED, losses to participants
  6. Wakalah: Wakeel (agent), gets FEE (not profit share)
  7. 3 Transaction Requirements (DID): Deliverable, Identified, Date determined
  8. Terminology: Participant (not insured), Contribution (not premium)

Chapter 9: Legal Principles (10-12%)

  1. 3 Principles: Utmost Good Faith, Insurable Interest, Proximate Cause
  2. 4 Utmost Faith Breaches: Non-disclosure, Concealment, Fraud, Innocent Misrepresentation
  3. Insurable Interest: LIFE = at inception, GENERAL = at time of loss
  4. 3 Authority Types: Express (written), Implied (necessary), Apparent (perceived)
  5. 5 Contract Characteristics (AUCPA): Aleatory, Unilateral, Conditional, Personal, Adhesion
  6. Agent filling forms = Acts for INSURER (not client)
  7. FSA 2013 = Replaced Insurance Act 1996

Chapter 10: Consumer Protection (6-8%)

  1. 8 Consumer Rights: Safety, Information, Choice, Be Heard, Redress, Education, Satisfaction, Basic Goods
  2. Agent Registration = LIAM (NOT NAMLIA - doesn’t exist!)
  3. Age Requirement = 18+
  4. Exam = PCEIA (life) or PCIL (general)
  5. CPD Hours: Life (20/30), General (12/20) - New/Existing
  6. Free-look Period = 15 days
  7. Free-look Refund = Full premium MINUS medical exam costs only
  8. Code of Good Practice = July 1, 1997 onwards

Chapter 11: SOCSO (5-7%)

  1. 2 Schemes: EIIS (work-related, no age limit), IPS (non-work, under 60)
  2. Contribution Rates: Employee 0.5%, Employer 1.75% (Total 2.25%)
  3. Exempted (SGDF): Self-employed, Government, Domestic, Foreign
  4. Contract Workers = COVERED (not exempted!)
  5. Wages INCLUDE: Salary, allowances, overtime, LEAVE PAYMENTS
  6. Wages EXCLUDE: Annual bonus, employer statutory contributions
  7. 2 Employer Types: Principal (hires + pays), Immediate (supervises)
  8. Can claim BOTH: SOCSO + other insurance (not exclusive)
  9. Appeals = Social Security Appellate Board (not SOCSO Board)

16 Two transaction conditions sufficient NO! Need all THREE
17 Agent acts for client when filling forms NO! Acts for insurer
18 Fixed annuity has no inflation risk NO! Has inflation risk

20 “After” includes the date mentioned NO! “On or after” does
