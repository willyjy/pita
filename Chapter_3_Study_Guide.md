## 💰 3.3 Life Insurance Valuation Methods

### Method 1: Human Life Value (HLV) Approach

**Concept**: Present value of future income that would be lost if person dies.

**Formula:**
```
HLV = A × (1 + r) × [(1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r]

Where:
A = Annual income for dependents
r = Interest rate (discount rate)
n = Years to retirement
```

**CRITICAL**: Set calculator to **BEGIN mode** (income received at start of year)

**Example 3-1: Mr. Aziz**
- Age: 30, Retirement: 55 (n = 25 years)
- Annual income: RM60,000
- Income for dependents: RM40,000 (2/3 of income)
- Interest rate: 6%

**Calculator Steps:**
1. Set to BEGIN mode
2. n = 25
3. i = 6
4. PMT = 40,000
5. Compute PV = **RM542,014.30**

**Interpretation**: RM542,014.30 is the total present value of income lost if Mr. Aziz dies prematurely.

---

**Limitations of HLV** (MUST KNOW for exam):

| Limitation | Reality |
|------------|---------|
| ❌ Assumes salary unchanged | ✅ Salary typically increases |
| ❌ Assumes constant interest rate | ✅ Rates fluctuate |
| ❌ Ignores inflation | ✅ Inflation erodes purchasing power |
| ❌ Ignores lifestyle changes | ✅ Needs change (marriage, children) |
| ❌ Assumes zero value at retirement | ✅ May have post-retirement income |
| ❌ HLV decreases as age increases | ✅ Actually may increase with salary growth |

⚠️ **EXAM TRAP**: Questions may present scenario where HLV seems to decrease with age, but ask you to identify this as a limitation.

📌 **EXAM TIP**: Despite limitations, HLV is "a good method to get started - at least there's a numerical value to focus on with client."

### Method 2: Capital Needs Approach

**Concept**: Calculate total capital needed to meet ALL financial needs of dependents.

**TWO Sub-Methods:**

#### A. Capital Liquidation Approach

**Concept**: Both **principal AND interest** consumed over time. Capital eventually depleted.

**Formula:**
```
Capital Needed = PV of income needs (using PVIFA)

Where income is consumed over fixed period
```

**Example: RM60,000 annual income, 7 years, 6% interest**

**Calculator Steps:**
1. Set to BEGIN mode
2. n = 7
3. i = 6
4. PMT = 60,000
5. Compute PV = **RM374,457**

**Interpretation**: RM374,457 needed if capital will be fully used up over 7 years.

---

#### B. Capital Conservation Approach

**Concept**: Only **interest** consumed. Principal **preserved intact** for heirs.

**Formula:**
```
Capital Needed = Annual Income Needed / Interest Rate

Capital Needed = A / r
```

**Example: RM60,000 annual income, 6% interest**
```
Capital Needed = RM60,000 / 0.06 = RM1,000,000
```

**Interpretation**: RM1,000,000 invested at 6% generates RM60,000 annually FOREVER, principal intact.

---

**Comparison Table (CRITICAL for Exam):**

| Aspect | Capital Liquidation | Capital Conservation |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Principal** | Consumed with interest | Preserved (not touched) |
| **Duration** | Fixed period only | Indefinite (perpetuity) |
| **Amount Needed** | Lower | Higher (always more) |
| **Purpose** | Income for dependents only | Income + Legacy for heirs |
| **Formula** | PVIFA calculation | A / r |
| **Example (RM60k, 6%)** | RM374,457 (7 years) | RM1,000,000 (forever) |

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: Capital Conservation ALWAYS requires MORE capital than Liquidation.

📌 **EXAM PATTERN**: Questions often ask "which requires more capital?" Answer: Conservation

### 3.3 Complete Capital Needs Calculation

**Total Life Insurance Need Formula:**

```
Total Need = [Cash Needs + Income Replacement + Legacy Needs] - Available Resources

Where:
Cash Needs = Debts + Funeral + Final expenses
Income Replacement = Liquidation OR Conservation amount
Legacy Needs = Charitable giving, inheritance
Available Resources = Cash, EPF, existing insurance, investments
```

**Example 3-4: Raymond Wong**

**Given:**
- Cash/equivalent resources: RM300,000
- Annual income needs: RM60,000 for 5 years
- Interest rate: 6%
- Cash needs at death: RM200,000
- Legacy (charity): RM80,000

**Method 1: Capital Liquidation**
```
Income Replacement (5 years, 6%):
PV = RM267,908 (calculator: BEGIN mode, n=5, i=6, PMT=60,000)

Total Need = [200,000 + 267,908 + 80,000] - 300,000
          = 547,908 - 300,000
          = RM247,908 (DEFICIENCY)
```

**Method 2: Capital Conservation**
```
Income Replacement (perpetual, 6%):
Capital = 60,000 / 0.06 = RM1,000,000

Total Need = [200,000 + 1,000,000 + 80,000] - 300,000
          = 1,280,000 - 300,000
          = RM980,000 (DEFICIENCY)
```

⚠️ **EXAM TIP**: Deficiency = Insurance amount to buy. If negative (surplus), no insurance needed for that component.

## 🏥 3.4 Critical Illness Coverage

**Growing Need in Malaysia:**
- Rising medical costs (10-15% annual increase)
- Common critical illnesses: Cancer, heart disease, stroke, kidney failure
- Private hospital costs: RM50,000-300,000+ per major treatment

**Valuation Method Components:**

| Component | Calculation | Malaysian Context |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Immediate Costs** | Hospital, surgery, initial treatment | RM50,000-100,000 typical |
| **Long-term Costs** | Follow-up, rehabilitation, modifications | RM20,000+ annually |
| **Income Replacement** | Lost income during recovery | (Monthly income × Recovery months) |
| **Inflation Adjustment** | Apply medical inflation rate | Use 10-15% (higher than general inflation) |
| **Existing Coverage** | Deduct current insurance/savings | Company medical, personal savings |
| **Lifestyle Costs** | Home care, dependent support | RM10,000-30,000 annually |

**Example 3-5: Abdullah Family**
```
Immediate medical: RM50,000
Ongoing treatment (annual): RM20,000
Income replacement (2 years): RM200,000 (RM100k × 2)
Lifestyle/care: RM10,000 annually
Less: Existing coverage: (RM30,000)
Less: Emergency savings: (RM50,000)

Total Critical Illness Need = RM200,000+
```

⚠️ **EXAM TIP**: Questions may ask to calculate total need - add all costs, subtract existing resources.

## 🦽 3.5 Disability Income Coverage

**Formula (Capital Conservation):**
```
Capital Needed = Annual Income to Replace / Interest Rate

Capital = A / r
```

**Example:**
- Annual income to replace: RM72,000
- Interest rate: 7%
- Capital Needed = 72,000 / 0.07 = **RM1,028,571**

**Important Considerations:**
- Disability often more financially devastating than death (ongoing costs)
- May need lifetime coverage (vs. death which is one-time)
- Include medical costs + income replacement
- Social Security (SOCSO) provides some disability coverage but often insufficient

## 🎓 3.7 Education Funding

**Approach**: Calculate future cost and work backwards to present value.

**Process:**
1. Estimate total education cost (university 4 years)
2. Apply education inflation (typically 6-8% in Malaysia)
3. Calculate lump sum needed TODAY to fund future cost
4. OR calculate annual savings needed

**Malaysian Context:**
- Local public university: RM30,000-50,000 total
- Local private university: RM100,000-200,000 total
- Overseas (UK/US): RM400,000-800,000 total

**Solutions:**
- Education insurance plans
- SSPN (National Education Savings Scheme)
- Life insurance with education rider
- Investment-linked plans

## 🏢 3.13 Business-Related Insurance Needs

**Key Risks for Business Owners:**

| Risk | Impact | Insurance Solution |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| **Death of Owner** | Business continuity, succession | Buy-sell agreement funded by life insurance |
| **Disability of Owner** | Business operations disrupted | Disability income, key person insurance |
| **Key Person Loss** | Loss of critical employee | Key man insurance |
| **Property Damage** | Business interruption | Business property, interruption insurance |
| **Employee Benefits** | Retirement, death gratuity obligations | Group insurance, retirement plans |

**Buy-Sell Agreement Funding:**
- Partners insure each other
- Upon death, proceeds buy deceased's share
- Ensures smooth transition, fair value for heirs

## 📊 3.10 Total Needs vs. Single Needs

**Critical Distinction:**

| Approach | Scenario | Calculation |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Single Needs** | Single individual, no dependents | Own debts + final expenses + personal goals |
| **Total Needs** | Married with dependents | Both spouses' income replacement + family obligations |

**Married Couples - TWO Approaches:**

**Option 1: Single Need Per Person**
- Calculate husband's need independently
- Calculate wife's need independently
- Buy separate policies

**Option 2: Total Combined Need**
- Calculate total family need if breadwinner dies
- May result in different coverage amounts
- Often more accurate for joint expenses

⚠️ **EXAM SCENARIO**: Question presents married couple, asks for insurance calculation.
- If asks for "husband's coverage" = Single need for husband
- If asks for "family protection" = Total needs approach
