## 📋 Must-Memorize Quick Lists

### Chapter 1: Risk Types
- Pure vs Speculative
- Subjective vs Objective
- Particular vs Fundamental
- Peril vs Hazard (3 types)

### Chapter 2: Risk Management
- 4 Techniques: Avoidance, Retention, Reduction, Transfer
- Law of Large Numbers
- Pooling concept

### Chapter 3: Needs Analysis
- HLV formula: (Annual Income × Years) - Funeral
- Capital Needs = Debts + Goals + Emergency
- Income Needs = Dependency Period × Annual Need

### Chapter 4: Life Insurance
- 3 Basic Types: Term, Whole Life, Endowment
- Non-forfeiture: Cash, RPT, EPT
- Settlement: Lump, Installment, Interest, Life Income

### Chapter 5: Medical Insurance
- Deductible, Co-insurance, Annual limit
- MRTA vs MLTA (reducing vs level)
- Waiting periods: 30 days, 120 days

### Chapter 6: Annuities
- Tax: RM3,000 deduction, exempt income (1995)
- 6 Types: Fixed/Variable, Immediate/Deferred, etc.
- 2 Phases: Accumulation, Liquidation

### Chapter 9: Legal
- 3 Principles: Utmost faith, Insurable interest, Proximate cause
- 3 Authority: Express, Implied, Apparent
- 5 Characteristics: Aleatory, Unilateral, Conditional, Personal, Adhesion

### Chapter 10: Consumer Protection
- 8 Rights: Safety, Info, Choice, Heard, Redress, Education, Satisfaction, Basic goods
- CPD: Life 20/30, General 12/20
- Free-look: 15 days, minus medical only

### Chapter 11: SOCSO
- Rates: Employee 0.5%, Employer 1.75%
- Exempted: Self-employed, Govt, Domestic, Foreign
- 2 Schemes: EIIS (work), IPS (non-work, under 60)

## 🎓 Study Tips

### For Calculations:
1. Write out formula first
2. Identify all variables
3. Show working step-by-step
4. Check answer makes sense

### For Concepts:
1. Understand the WHY (not just memorize)
2. Use comparison tables
3. Create mental associations
4. Teach concept to someone else

### For Exam Day:
1. Read question carefully (keywords!)
2. Eliminate obviously wrong answers
3. Watch for "NOT" or "EXCEPT"
4. Check if asking for single or multiple items
5. Time management: ~1 minute per question

## ⚠️ Common Exam Traps Across All Chapters

| Trap Type | Example | Reality |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Similar Terms** | Pure vs Speculative | Different risk types |
| **Close Numbers** | 0.5% vs 0.75% | Exact rates matter |
| **Reversed Concepts** | MRTA vs MLTA | MRTA reduces, MLTA level |
| **Excluded Items** | What's NOT covered | Read carefully |
| **Timing Issues** | When insurable interest needed | Life=inception, General=loss |
| **Organizations** | LIAM vs NAMLIA | NAMLIA doesn't exist! |
| **One vs Multiple** | Can claim one or both | Often can claim both |
| **Age Limits** | Different for different schemes | EIIS=none, IPS=under 60 |
