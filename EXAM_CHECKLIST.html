✅ Chapter 4: Life Insurance Policies (15-18%) - HIGHEST WEIGHT

File: Chapter_4_Study_Guide.md - 77KB | 30 Questions

MRTA (Mortgage Reducing Term):

✅ Chapter 7: General Insurance Products (12-15%)

File: Chapter_7_Study_Guide.md - 31KB | 10 Questions

Must Know: - [ ] 3 Principles: Indemnity, Contribution, Subrogation - [ ] 4 Motor coverage levels (Act, TP, TPFT, Comprehensive) - [ ] NCD rates (25%, 30%, 38.33%, 45%, 55%) - [ ] 3 Construction Bonds (TAP): Tender, Advance Payment, Performance - [ ] CAR vs EAR vs Machinery Breakdown - [ ] Employer’s Liability vs Workmen’s Comp

Motor Insurance:

ACT: Third-party death/injury only (minimum legal)
THIRD PARTY: + property damage
TPFT: + fire & theft (own vehicle)
COMPREHENSIVE: All of above + own vehicle accident damage

Legal: - Mandatory under RTA 1987 - Penalty: RM1,000 fine + 3 months jail + 12-month disqualification

Construction Bonds (TAP): - Tender Bond (bidding stage) - Advance Payment Bond (protect upfront payment) - Performance Bond (ensure completion)

✅ Chapter 11: SOCSO (5-7%)

File: Chapter_11_Study_Guide.md - 28KB | 10 Questions

Must Know: - [ ] 2 Schemes: EIIS (work-related), IPS (non-work) - [ ] Contribution rates: Employee 0.5%, Employer 1.75% - [ ] Exempted categories (SGDF) - [ ] Age limits (EIIS: none, IPS: under 60) - [ ] Employer types: Principal, Immediate - [ ] Wages definition (include/exclude)

Two Schemes:

EIIS (Employment Injury Insurance):
- Work-related injuries/diseases
- NO age limit
- Commuting accidents included

IPS (Invalidity Pension):
- Non-work-related disabilities
- Must be UNDER 60 at diagnosis
- Death any cause

Contribution Rates (CRITICAL): - Employee: 0.5% - Employer: 1.75% - Total: 2.25%

Exempted (SGDF): - Self-employed - Government servants - Domestic workers - Foreign workers

Wages: - INCLUDE: Salary, allowances, overtime, leave payments - EXCLUDE: Annual bonus, employer statutory contributions

⚠️ TOP 20 EXAM TRAPS

  1. Contract workers → COVERED by SOCSO (not exempted)
  2. NAMLIA → Does NOT exist (it’s LIAM)
  3. MRTA vs MLTA → MRTA reduces, MLTA level
  4. Age 60 SOCSO → IPS stops at 60 (not continue)
  5. Can claim both → SOCSO + other insurance (not exclusive)
  6. 0.5% employee → Not 0.75% (don’t confuse with EPF)
  7. Bonus = wages → Annual bonus EXCLUDED
  8. Leave payments → INCLUDED in SOCSO wages
  9. NCD certificate → Must get from previous insurer
  10. Digital access → NOT one of 8 basic consumer rights
  11. Banking Security Bond → NOT construction bond (only TAP)
  12. Employee negligence → NOT covered in Employer’s Liability
  13. CAR during operation → CAR for installation, Machinery for operation
  14. Guaranteed Takaful profits → NO guarantees in Islamic finance
  15. Wakalah = profit share → Wakalah gets FEE only
  16. Two transaction conditions → ALL THREE required (DID)
  17. Agent acts for client → Acts for INSURER when filling forms
  18. Fixed annuity no inflation risk → Has inflation risk
  19. Pure life to heirs → NOTHING to heirs (fully liquidated)
  20. After vs On/After → “On or after” is inclusive

📈 EXAM STRATEGY

Time Management:

  • Total questions: ~100 (varies by exam)
  • Time: ~90-120 minutes
  • Per question: ~1 minute
  • Mark difficult questions, return later
  • Leave 10 minutes for review

Question Approach:

  1. Read carefully - Watch for “NOT”, “EXCEPT”, “FALSE”
  2. Identify keywords - Highlight key terms
  3. Eliminate wrong answers - Cross out obviously wrong
  4. Check if single or multiple - “Which is/are”
  5. Don’t overthink - First instinct often correct

During Exam:

  • Stay calm
  • Read each question fully
  • Watch for negatives (“NOT”, “EXCEPT”)
  • Manage time (don’t spend too long on one)
  • Review answers if time permits
