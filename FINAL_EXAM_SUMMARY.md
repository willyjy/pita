## 🔥 TOP 50 MUST-KNOW FACTS (By Chapter)

### Chapter 1: Understanding Risks (8-10%)

1. **Pure Risk** = Loss or no loss (INSURABLE)
2. **Speculative Risk** = Profit, loss, or no loss (NOT insurable)
3. **Peril** = Cause of loss (fire, theft, death)
4. **Hazard** = Condition increasing probability/severity
5. **3 Hazard Types**: Physical (tangible), Moral (dishonesty), Morale (carelessness)

---

### Chapter 2: Risk Management (12-15%)

6. **4 Risk Techniques (ARRT)**: Avoidance, Retention, Reduction, Transfer
7. **Insurance = TRANSFER** technique
8. **Law of Large Numbers** = More exposures → More predictable losses
9. **Pooling** = Spread losses among many → Reduce individual impact
10. **Risk Management Process**: Identify → Measure → Analyze → Control → Communicate

---

### Chapter 3: Needs Analysis (6-8%)

11. **HLV Formula** = (Annual Income × Years to Retirement) - Funeral Costs
12. **Capital Needs** = Debts + Education + Emergency + Goals
13. **Income Needs** = Dependency Period × Annual Income Need
14. **Emergency Fund** = 6-12 months expenses
15. **Dependency Period** = Years until youngest child independent

---

### Chapter 4: Life Insurance (15-18%) - HIGHEST WEIGHT

16. **3 Basic Types**: TERM (pure protection), WHOLE LIFE (lifetime), ENDOWMENT (savings+protection)
17. **Term** = Lowest premium, no cash value
18. **Whole Life** = Level premium, builds cash value, lifetime coverage
19. **Endowment** = Highest premium, guaranteed maturity, high cash value
20. **3 Non-forfeiture Options**: CASH, RPT (Reduced Paid-up Term), EPT (Extended Paid-up Term)
21. **4 Settlement Options**: Lump Sum, Installment, Interest Only, Life Income
22. **Grace Period** = 30 days (annual) / 7 days (other modes)
23. **Reinstatement** = Within 2 years
24. **Incontestability** = 2 years (cannot contest after)
25. **Suicide Clause** = 1 year (no payout if suicide within)

---

### Chapter 5: Medical Insurance (10-12%)

26. **MRTA** = Reducing coverage, level premium (for LOANS)
27. **MLTA** = Level coverage, level premium (more protection)
28. **Deductible** = You pay first (e.g., first RM500)
29. **Co-insurance** = Share % after deductible (e.g., 80/20)
30. **Annual Limit** = Maximum per year
31. **Waiting Periods**: General (30 days), Pre-existing (120 days)
32. **Medical Card** = Room & Board + scheduled benefits
33. **H&S** = Indemnity, actual expenses

---

### Chapter 6: Annuities (8-10%)

34. **Annuity** = Reverse life insurance (protection against living too long)
35. **2 Phases**: Accumulation (pay in), Liquidation (pay out)
36. **Fixed Annuity** = Guaranteed payments, has inflation risk
37. **Variable Annuity** = Payments vary with investment, inflation hedge
38. **Pure Life Annuity** = NOTHING to heirs (highest payment)
39. **Annuity Certain** = Guaranteed period (something to heirs)
40. **Malaysia Tax**: RM3,000 deduction (2012-2025), Income exempt (since 1995)

---

### Chapter 7: General Insurance (12-15%)

41. **3 Principles**: INDEMNITY (restore original), CONTRIBUTION (share among insurers), SUBROGATION (recover from third party)
42. **Motor Levels**: Act < Third Party < TPFT < Comprehensive
43. **NCD Rates**: 25% → 30% → 38.33% → 45% → 55% (max)
44. **RTA 1987** = Motor insurance MANDATORY
45. **Penalty**: RM1,000 fine + 3 months jail + 12-month disqualification
46. **3 Construction Bonds (TAP)**: Tender (bidding), Advance Payment (upfront $), Performance (completion)
47. **CAR** = During INSTALLATION/construction
48. **Machinery Breakdown** = During OPERATION (after installed)
49. **Employer's Liability** = Covers EMPLOYER'S fault only (not employee's negligence)
50. **Workmen's Comp** = For workmen WITHOUT SOCSO

---

### Chapter 8: Takaful (8-10%)

51. **3 Forbidden (GMR)**: Gharar (uncertainty), Maisir (gambling), Riba (interest)
52. **Kafala** = Root word meaning "to guarantee"
53. **Tabarru** = Donation to mutual pool
54. **2 Models (MW)**: Mudharabah (profit-sharing), Wakalah (agency)
55. **Mudharabah**: Mudarib (entrepreneur), profits SHARED, losses to participants
56. **Wakalah**: Wakeel (agent), gets FEE (not profit share)
57. **3 Transaction Requirements (DID)**: Deliverable, Identified, Date determined
58. **Terminology**: Participant (not insured), Contribution (not premium)

---

### Chapter 9: Legal Principles (10-12%)

59. **3 Principles**: Utmost Good Faith, Insurable Interest, Proximate Cause
60. **4 Utmost Faith Breaches**: Non-disclosure, Concealment, Fraud, Innocent Misrepresentation
61. **Insurable Interest**: LIFE = at inception, GENERAL = at time of loss
62. **3 Authority Types**: Express (written), Implied (necessary), Apparent (perceived)
63. **5 Contract Characteristics (AUCPA)**: Aleatory, Unilateral, Conditional, Personal, Adhesion
64. **Agent filling forms** = Acts for INSURER (not client)
65. **FSA 2013** = Replaced Insurance Act 1996

---

### Chapter 10: Consumer Protection (6-8%)

66. **8 Consumer Rights**: Safety, Information, Choice, Be Heard, Redress, Education, Satisfaction, Basic Goods
67. **Agent Registration** = LIAM (NOT NAMLIA - doesn't exist!)
68. **Age Requirement** = 18+
69. **Exam** = PCEIA (life) or PCIL (general)
70. **CPD Hours**: Life (20/30), General (12/20) - New/Existing
71. **Free-look Period** = 15 days
72. **Free-look Refund** = Full premium MINUS medical exam costs only
73. **Code of Good Practice** = July 1, 1997 onwards

---

### Chapter 11: SOCSO (5-7%)

74. **2 Schemes**: EIIS (work-related, no age limit), IPS (non-work, under 60)
75. **Contribution Rates**: Employee 0.5%, Employer 1.75% (Total 2.25%)
76. **Exempted (SGDF)**: Self-employed, Government, Domestic, Foreign
77. **Contract Workers** = COVERED (not exempted!)
78. **Wages INCLUDE**: Salary, allowances, overtime, LEAVE PAYMENTS
79. **Wages EXCLUDE**: Annual bonus, employer statutory contributions
80. **2 Employer Types**: Principal (hires + pays), Immediate (supervises)
81. **Can claim BOTH**: SOCSO + other insurance (not exclusive)
82. **Appeals** = Social Security Appellate Board (not SOCSO Board)
