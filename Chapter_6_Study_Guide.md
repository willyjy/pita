## 📖 Complete Chapter Summary

### 6.0 Introduction

**The Core Problem**: Risk of "Living Too Long" and outliving savings

**Solution**: Annuities = systematic liquidation of capital to provide lifetime income

---

**🔑 KEY CONCEPT - The Opposite of Life Insurance:**

| Product | Protects Against | Focus | Payment Direction |
|---------|-----------------|-------|-------------------|
| **Life Insurance** | Dying TOO SOON (premature death) | Mortality risk | You pay premiums → Insurer pays beneficiaries on death |
| **Annuity** | Living TOO LONG (outliving savings) | Longevity risk | You pay lump sum → Insurer pays you periodic income |

📌 **EXAM TIP**: Annuity = **REVERSE** life insurance. Life insurance converts small payments into large sum; annuity converts large sum into small periodic payments.

---

**Malaysian Context:**

| Status | Details | Reason |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Current Popularity** | LOW - not actively promoted | Limited public awareness |
| **Future Trend** | Expected to GROW | Tax changes, aging population, EPF concerns |
| **Global Comparison** | USA, Japan = Very popular | Aging population, inflation concerns, retirement needs |

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: While not currently popular in Malaysia, annuities are expected to become more important as population ages.

A. Whole life policy with profits

**Answer**: C (A life annuity)

**🔑 KEY INSIGHT**: For **RETIREMENT INCOME** needs, annuity is the BEST solution!

- ✅ **Provides LIFETIME income** (can't outlive it)
- ✅ **Regular payments** for living expenses
- ✅ **Specifically designed** for retirement years
- ✅ **Protects against longevity risk**

**WHY OTHERS ARE LESS SUITABLE**:
- **A. Whole Life**: Pays on **DEATH**, not for retirement income
  - Benefit goes to beneficiaries, not you
  - Doesn't provide regular income stream

- **B. Endowment (age 60)**: ONE-TIME lump sum at maturity
  - What if you live to 90? (30 more years!)
  - Must manage lump sum yourself
  - Can outlive the money

**KEYWORDS**:
- "Retirement years" = Need **INCOME** ✅
- "Most suited" = Annuity = **PURPOSE-BUILT** for retirement ✅
- "Life annuity" = **LIFETIME** payments ✅

**MEMORY TIP**: 
```
RETIREMENT = Need INCOME (not lump sum)
INCOME for LIFE = ANNUITY
```

### 6.1 Understanding Annuities

**Definition**: Contract with insurer where insurer makes periodic payments to annuitant, starting immediately or future date, for lifetime or specified term.

**Formal Definition** (memorize):
> "An annuity is a contract with an insurance company providing periodic payments to the annuitant for a lifetime or specified term, serving as reverse life insurance focusing on longevity risk."

---

### 6.1.a Function of Annuities

**Primary Function**: Mitigate **LONGEVITY RISK**

**What is Longevity Risk?**
- Risk of outliving your assets
- Living longer than expected
- Running out of money in old age

**How Annuities Help:**
- Convert lump sum → steady income stream
- Provide financial security
- Give peace of mind (guaranteed income)
- Can't outlive payments (if lifetime annuity)

---

**📝 EXAM QUESTION 2** *(Links to Section 6.1.a - Function)*

**Question**: "What is basic function of annuity?
A. To provide lifetime replacement or supplementary income immediately ✅
B. To provide inflation adjusted living during retirement
C. To protect against medical expenses in old age
D. To guarantee high income and enjoyable retirement"

**Answer**: A (Provide lifetime replacement/supplementary income)

**🔑 KEY INSIGHT**: Basic function = **INCOME PROVISION**, nothing fancy!

**ANALYSIS OF EACH OPTION**:

**A** ✅ **CORRECT** - The BASIC function:
- **"Lifetime"** = Core feature (longevity protection)
- **"Replacement or supplementary"** = Can replace work income OR supplement other income
- **"Immediately"** = For immediate annuities (can also be deferred)
- This is the **FUNDAMENTAL purpose**

**B** ❌ WRONG - Too specific:
- Inflation adjustment is **OPTIONAL feature**, not basic function
- Not all annuities are inflation-adjusted
- This is a **NICE-TO-HAVE**, not core purpose

**C** ❌ WRONG - Wrong product!
- Medical expenses = **HEALTH INSURANCE** function
- Annuities = **INCOME**, not medical coverage
- Completely different purpose

**D** ❌ WRONG - Too optimistic/unrealistic:
- "Guarantee high income" = **FALSE** (depends on amount invested)
- "Enjoyable retirement" = Subjective, not insurance function
- Overpromises what annuity can do

**KEYWORDS**:
- "Basic function" = **CORE purpose** (income provision)
- "Lifetime" = **LONGEVITY protection**
- "Replacement/supplementary" = **INCOME focus**

**EXAM TIP**: Don't be distracted by fancy features (inflation, medical, "enjoyable"). Basic function = **PROVIDE INCOME**.

### 6.1.b Life Cycle of Annuities

**TWO PHASES** (CRITICAL to understand):

```
ANNUITY LIFE CYCLE

Phase 1: ACCUMULATION → Phase 2: LIQUIDATION
(Building)              (Paying Out)
```

---

#### 6.1.b.i Accumulation Phase

**Definition**: Period of capital investment into annuity.

**What Happens:**

| Activity | Description | Result |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Premium Payment** | Annuity holder pays premiums | Fund grows |
| **Payment Type** | Lump-sum OR periodic | Flexibility |
| **Interest Accrual** | Invested amount earns interest | Principal increases |
| **Investment Growth** | Based on underlying investment strategy | Fund accumulation |

**Example:**
```
Age 30-60 (30 years accumulation):
Monthly premium: RM500
Total paid: RM500 × 12 × 30 = RM180,000
With 5% annual return: Fund grows to ~RM416,000
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: Accumulation phase = **PAY IN** period (you're building the fund).

---

#### 6.1.b.ii Liquidation Phase

**Definition**: Period where accumulated funds distributed back to annuitant.

**What Happens:**

| Activity | Description | Result |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Payment Commencement** | Regular payments begin | Income stream starts |
| **Payment Structure** | Fixed period OR lifetime | Depends on annuity type |
| **Fund Depletion** | Systematic withdrawal of capital | Provides income |
| **Longevity Protection** | Payments continue (lifetime annuity) | Can't outlive |

**Example:**
```
Age 60+ (liquidation begins):
Accumulated fund: RM416,000
Monthly payment: RM3,000 (calculated actuarially)
Duration: Lifetime (even if live to 100+)
If live 30 years: Receive RM3,000 × 12 × 30 = RM1,080,000!
(Much more than paid in due to pooling and mortality credits)
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: Liquidation phase = **PAY OUT** period (insurer pays you income).

---

**Comparison Table:**

| Aspect | Accumulation Phase | Liquidation Phase |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Direction** | Money IN (you → insurer) | Money OUT (insurer → you) |
| **Duration** | Variable (years/decades) | Fixed period OR lifetime |
| **Purpose** | Build capital | Provide income |
| **Who Benefits** | Future you | Current you |
| **Risk** | Investment risk | Longevity risk covered |

### 6.1.c Classification of Annuities

**FIVE Classification Bases** (memorize):

1. **Number of Lives Covered**
   - Single life
   - Multiple lives (e.g., husband & wife)

2. **Method of Premium Payment**
   - Single premium
   - Periodic premiums

3. **Timing of Income Payments**
   - Immediate (starts now)
   - Deferred (starts later)

4. **Method of Disposing Proceeds**
   - Immediate distribution
   - Deferred to specific date
   - Payments to certain age (e.g., age 65)

5. **Denomination of Benefits**
   - Various currencies (USD, RM, etc.)

📌 **EXAM TIP**: These classifications form the basis for understanding different annuity types.

#### 6.1.d.i Fixed-Sum vs Variable Annuity

**Fixed-Sum (Fixed) Annuity:**

| Feature | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Payment Amount** | FIXED, predetermined | RM2,000/month (never changes) |
| **Guarantee** | Amount guaranteed | Certainty of income |
| **Inflation Risk** | HIGH - purchasing power erodes | RM2,000 today ≠ RM2,000 in 20 years |
| **Investment Risk** | LOW - insurer bears risk | Safe but inflexible |
| **Suitability** | Conservative, risk-averse | Those wanting certainty |

**Example:**
```
Fixed Annuity: RM2,000/month
Year 1: RM2,000 (buys 1,000 eggs @ RM2 each)
Year 20: RM2,000 (inflation: eggs now RM4, buys only 500 eggs)
Result: Same nominal amount, LESS real purchasing power
```

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: Fixed annuities = **INFLATION RISK** (purchasing power erodes over time).

---

**Variable Annuity:**

| Feature | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Payment Amount** | VARIES based on investment performance | RM2,000-4,000/month (fluctuates) |
| **Guarantee** | NO guarantee on amount | Uncertainty |
| **Inflation Risk** | LOWER - can keep pace with inflation | Payments may increase |
| **Investment Risk** | HIGH - annuitant bears risk | Market exposure |
| **Suitability** | Growth-oriented, can handle volatility | Those wanting potential growth |

**Example:**
```
Variable Annuity tied to equity index:
Good year: RM3,500/month (index up 15%)
Bad year: RM1,800/month (index down 10%)
Average: ~RM2,500/month (better than fixed over long term if markets grow)
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: 
- **Fixed** = Certainty but inflation risk
- **Variable** = Growth potential but market risk

---

#### 6.1.d.ii Immediate vs Deferred Annuity

**Immediate Annuity:**

| Feature | Description | Malaysian Context |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Payment Start** | IMMEDIATELY (within 1 year) | Right after purchase |
| **Purchase** | Lump sum only | EPF lump sum, inheritance, retirement gratuity |
| **Accumulation Phase** | NONE | No growth period |
| **Typical Buyer** | Already retired, needs income NOW | Age 60+ |

**Example:**
```
Mr. Tan retires at 60:
EPF lump sum: RM500,000
Buys immediate annuity: RM3,500/month for life
Payments start: Next month
No accumulation needed - income begins immediately
```

---

**Deferred Annuity:**

| Feature | Description | Malaysian Context |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Payment Start** | LATER (after accumulation period) | 10-30 years later |
| **Purchase** | Single OR periodic premiums | Regular savings during working years |
| **Accumulation Phase** | YES - fund grows | Important growth period |
| **Typical Buyer** | Still working, planning ahead | Age 30-50 |

**Example:**
```
Ms. Lee age 35:
Buys deferred annuity: RM500/month premium
Accumulation: Age 35-60 (25 years)
Fund grows with interest
Payments start: Age 60
Monthly income: RM4,000/month for life
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: 
- **Immediate** = For those who need income **NOW** (already retired)
- **Deferred** = For those **PLANNING AHEAD** (still working)

---

**Historical Note - Malaysia:**
```
EPF Life Annuity Scheme (discontinued):
- Launched to convert EPF to lifetime income
- Two versions: Conventional & Takaful
- Both immediate and deferred options
- Discontinued due to pricing, administration, sales practice issues
```

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: EPF annuity scheme existed but **DISCONTINUED** - know this for Malaysian context questions.

#### 6.1.d.iv Single Payment vs Instalment Annuity

**Single Payment (Single Premium) Annuity:**

| Feature | Immediate Annuity | Deferred Annuity |
|---------|------------------|------------------|
| **Payment** | ONE lump sum | ONE lump sum |
| **Income Start** | Immediately | After deferral period |
| **Refund if Death** | N/A (already paying out) | Purchase price refundable (with/without interest) |
| **Surrender Value** | N/A | Allowed during deferral |

**Example:**
```
Immediate Single Premium:
Pay: RM500,000 (one time)
Receive: RM3,500/month immediately

Deferred Single Premium:
Pay: RM200,000 (one time, age 40)
Wait: 20 years (accumulation with interest)
Receive: RM4,000/month starting age 60
```

---

**Instalment (Periodic Premium) Annuity:**

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Payment** | Regular premiums over time (monthly, annually) |
| **Nature** | ALWAYS deferred (need time to accumulate) |
| **Refund if Death** | Premiums paid refundable during accumulation |
| **Surrender Value** | Available during accumulation period |
| **Flexibility** | Can adjust payments (some plans) |

**Example:**
```
Instalment Annuity:
Pay: RM500/month for 25 years (age 35-60)
Total paid: RM150,000
Fund grows: ~RM300,000 (with interest)
Receive: RM3,000/month starting age 60

If death at age 45 (during accumulation):
Beneficiaries receive: Premiums paid + interest
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: Instalment plans are **ALWAYS DEFERRED** (by nature, need time to pay installments).

---

#### 6.1.d.v Single Life vs Joint Life Annuity

**Single Life Annuity:**

| Feature | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Coverage** | ONE person only | Mr. Tan only |
| **Payment Duration** | As long as annuitant lives | Until Mr. Tan dies |
| **Death of Annuitant** | Payments STOP | No more payments to anyone |
| **Cost** | Lower (shorter expected duration) | Pays for one life |

**Example:**
```
Mr. Tan, age 60:
Buys single life annuity: RM3,500/month
Lives to 85 (25 years): Receives RM3,500 × 12 × 25 = RM1,050,000
Dies at 85: Payments STOP, nothing to wife/children
```

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: Single life = Payments stop at death, **NOTHING** to beneficiaries (unless annuity certain - see later).

---

**Joint-Life Annuity:**

| Feature | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Coverage** | TWO people (usually spouses) | Mr. & Mrs. Tan |
| **Payment Duration** | Until FIRST death | Stops when EITHER dies |
| **Death of First** | Payments STOP completely | Surviving spouse gets NOTHING |
| **Cost** | Moderate | Shorter than survivorship |

**Example:**
```
Mr. & Mrs. Tan:
Joint-life annuity: RM4,000/month
Mr. Tan dies age 75: Payments STOP
Mrs. Tan (still alive age 72): NO MORE PAYMENTS
Problem: Left survivor without income!
```

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: Joint-life = Stops at **FIRST** death (not ideal for protecting survivor).

---

**Survivorship Annuity (Last-to-Die):**

| Feature | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Coverage** | TWO people | Mr. & Mrs. Tan |
| **Payment Duration** | Until LAST death | Continues as long as ONE alive |
| **Death of First** | Payments CONTINUE to survivor | Protects surviving spouse |
| **Cost** | HIGHEST (longest expected duration) | Pays longest |
| **Variation** | Reduced payment after first death (e.g., 75% of original) | Common structure |

**Example:**
```
Mr. & Mrs. Tan:
Survivorship annuity: RM4,000/month
Mr. Tan dies age 75: Payments CONTINUE
Mrs. Tan receives: RM4,000/month (or reduced to RM3,000)
Until Mrs. Tan dies age 88
Total duration: 28 years (age 60-88)
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: **Survivorship = Most expensive** but **BEST protection** for couples (survivor continues to receive income).

---

**📝 EXAM QUESTION 5** *(Links to Section 6.1.d.v - Joint Life vs Survivorship)*

**Question**: "For ___, annuity payments cease on first death. For ___, annuity payments until death of last annuitant.
A. survivorship annuity; joint-life
B. joint-life; survivorship annuity ✅
C. Single life annuity; joint life annuity
D. Immediate life annuity; survivorship annuity"

**Answer**: B (joint-life; survivorship annuity)

**🔑 KEY INSIGHT**: Distinguish between **FIRST** death vs **LAST** death!

**ANALYSIS**:

**First blank: "cease on FIRST death"**
- = **JOINT-LIFE** annuity ✅
- When EITHER person dies (first death), payments STOP
- Survivor gets NOTHING

**Second blank: "until death of LAST annuitant"**
- = **SURVIVORSHIP** annuity ✅
- Payments continue as long as ONE person alive
- Stops only when BOTH dead (last death)

**MEMORY TIP**:
```
JOINT-LIFE = JOINS together, DIES together
(Stops at FIRST death - both must be alive)

SURVIVORSHIP = SURVIVOR continues
(Continues until LAST death - protects survivor)
```

**VISUAL AID**:
```
Joint-Life:
Husband dies → STOP ❌
Wife still alive, but no payments

Survivorship:
Husband dies → CONTINUE ✅
Wife still receives payments
Until wife dies → STOP
```

**KEYWORDS**:
- "Cease on FIRST death" = **JOINT-LIFE**
- "Until LAST annuitant" = **SURVIVORSHIP**

#### 6.1.d.vi Pure Life vs Annuity Certain

**Pure Life Annuity (Straight Life Annuity):**

| Feature | Description | Risk/Benefit |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Payment Duration** | Lifetime of annuitant ONLY | Can't outlive ✅ |
| **At Death** | Payments STOP immediately | Nothing to heirs ❌ |
| **Fully Liquidated** | Fund considered fully used up | No residual value ❌ |
| **Payment Amount** | HIGHEST (for same premium) | Maximum income ✅ |
| **Gamble** | If die early, lose money; if live long, win | Longevity bet |

**Example:**
```
Mr. Ahmad age 60:
Pure life annuity: RM4,000/month
Paid: RM500,000 premium

Scenario 1: Dies age 62 (2 years)
Received: RM4,000 × 12 × 2 = RM96,000
Lost: RM404,000 (insurer keeps) ❌

Scenario 2: Lives age 90 (30 years)
Received: RM4,000 × 12 × 30 = RM1,440,000
Gained: RM940,000 more than paid! ✅

Scenario 3: Dies age 70 (10 years)
Received: RM480,000
About break-even
```

⚠️ **EXAM ALERT**: Pure life = **HIGHEST** monthly payment but **NOTHING** to beneficiaries at death.

---

**📝 EXAM QUESTION 4** *(Links to Section 6.1.d.vi - Pure Life Annuity)*

**Question**: "Ah Seng purchased annuity, receives payments as long as he lives. Amount invested fully liquidated on death. This is:
A. Pure life annuity ✅
B. Deferred annuity
C. Instalment annuity
D. Annuity certain"

**Answer**: A (Pure life annuity)

**🔑 KEY INSIGHT**: Two key phrases identify PURE LIFE:
1. "As long as he lives" = **LIFETIME** payments
2. "Fully liquidated on death" = **NOTHING** left for heirs

**KEYWORD ANALYSIS**:

✅ **"Receives payments as long as he lives"**
- = LIFETIME duration
- Continues until death
- Can't outlive payments

✅ **"Amount invested fully liquidated on death"**
- = NO residual value
- Nothing to beneficiaries
- Fund completely used up
- This is THE defining feature of PURE LIFE!

**WHY NOT OTHERS**:

❌ **B. Deferred** - This refers to WHEN payments START (immediate vs deferred), not the TYPE of payout structure

❌ **C. Instalment** - This refers to HOW PREMIUM IS PAID (single vs periodic), not payout characteristics

❌ **D. Annuity Certain** - This has GUARANTEED period payments regardless of life/death (opposite of pure life)

**MEMORY TIP**:
```
PURE LIFE = PURE gamble on longevity
- Live long = WIN BIG ✅
- Die early = LOSE ❌
- Nothing left = PURE (nothing for heirs)
```

---

**Annuity Certain (Period Certain / Term Certain):**

| Feature | Description | Risk/Benefit |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Payment Duration** | FIXED period regardless of life/death | Guaranteed minimum ✅ |
| **If Death During Period** | Payments CONTINUE to beneficiaries | Heirs protected ✅ |
| **NOT Life-Dependent** | Payments made regardless of survival | Certainty ✅ |
| **Payment Amount** | LOWER (for same premium) | Trade-off for guarantee ❌ |
| **Types** | Can be immediate OR deferred | Flexible |

**Common Structures:**

1. **Life Annuity with Period Certain (e.g., 10 years certain)**
```
Mr. Lee age 60:
Life annuity with 10 years certain: RM3,500/month

Scenario 1: Dies age 65 (5 years)
Payments continue to beneficiaries: 5 more years
Total paid: 10 years guaranteed

Scenario 2: Lives to 85 (25 years)
Payments continue: All 25 years (beyond 10-year guarantee)
Protects against both early death AND longevity
```

2. **Installment Refund Annuity**
```
Premium paid: RM500,000
Monthly payment: RM3,000

Dies after receiving: RM180,000 (5 years)
Balance refunded to heirs: RM320,000
Ensures premium fully returned
```

3. **Cash Refund Annuity**
```
Similar to installment refund but balance paid as LUMP SUM
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: "Certain" = **GUARANTEED** period, payments continue even if annuitant dies.

---

**Comparison Table: Pure Life vs Annuity Certain**

| Feature | Pure Life | Annuity Certain |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| **At Death** | Payments STOP | Payments CONTINUE (if within certain period) |
| **Beneficiaries** | Get NOTHING | Get remaining payments/balance |
| **Monthly Payment** | HIGHEST | LOWER |
| **Longevity Protection** | YES | YES (plus minimum guarantee) |
| **Early Death Risk** | HIGH (lose money) | LOW (protected) |
| **Suitability** | Healthy, no heirs concern | Family protection important |

**📝 EXAM QUESTION 3** *(Links to Section 6.1.d - Types of Annuities)*

**Question**: "Which is generally true of annuity?
A. For those who expect to live long, annuity not good option
B. Computation exactly same as life insurance
C. Lump sum deposited with insurer in exchange for stream of payments ✅
D. Younger person receives larger annuity payment than older"

**Answer**: C (Lump sum deposited for stream of payments)

**🔑 KEY INSIGHT**: This is the **FUNDAMENTAL DEFINITION** of how annuities work!

**ANALYSIS OF EACH OPTION**:

**C** ✅ **CORRECT** - This IS what annuity does:
- **"Lump sum deposited"** = You give money upfront (or accumulate)
- **"Exchange for"** = Contract trade
- **"Stream of payments"** = Regular periodic income
- **"In future"** = Over time (liquidation phase)
- This is the **ESSENCE** of annuity concept!

**A** ❌ **FALSE** - Complete OPPOSITE of truth!
- Annuities are PERFECT for those expecting to live long
- Live longer = Get more total payments
- Protects against outliving savings
- This statement is BACKWARDS!

**B** ❌ **FALSE** - Computation is OPPOSITE:
- **Life Insurance**: Small premiums → Large lump sum (mortality focus)
- **Annuity**: Large lump sum → Small periodic payments (longevity focus)
- Actuarial methods related but NOT the same

**D** ❌ **FALSE** - OPPOSITE is true:
- **OLDER person** receives **LARGER** payment (shorter life expectancy)
- **YOUNGER person** receives **SMALLER** payment (longer life expectancy)
- Same fund must last longer for younger = smaller per payment

**Example Proof (Why D is wrong)**:
```
Fund: RM500,000

Age 60 annuitant:
Life expectancy: 20 more years
Payment: RM500,000 / 20 years = RM2,083/month

Age 40 annuitant:
Life expectancy: 40 more years
Payment: RM500,000 / 40 years = RM1,042/month

OLDER person gets LARGER payment (same fund, shorter expected duration)
```

**KEYWORDS**:
- "Lump sum" = **UPFRONT capital**
- "Stream of payments" = **REGULAR income**
- "Exchange" = **CONVERSION** process

**MEMORY TIP**:
```
ANNUITY = OPPOSITE of saving
SAVING: Little by little → Build big amount
ANNUITY: Big amount → Distribute little by little
```

### 6.2 Tax Treatment of Annuity in Malaysia

**Regulatory Framework**: Public Ruling No. 4/2014 by Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (LHDN)

**THREE Key Tax Benefits:**

---

#### 6.2.a Deductibility of Premiums

**Tax Relief Details:**

| Aspect | Details | Exam Importance |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Amount** | Up to RM3,000 per year | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Eligible Products** | Deferred annuity OR PRS (Private Retirement Scheme) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Eligibility** | Malaysian residents only | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Period** | Year of assessment 2012-2025 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Purpose** | Encourage retirement savings | ⭐⭐⭐ |

**Example Calculation:**
```
Mr. Tan's income tax calculation:

Chargeable income: RM100,000
Tax without relief: ~RM14,000

Deferred annuity premium paid: RM5,000
Tax relief claimed: RM3,000 (maximum)

New chargeable income: RM100,000 - RM3,000 = RM97,000
Tax with relief: ~RM13,400
Savings: RM600 (immediate tax benefit!)
```

📌 **EXAM TIP**: Maximum deductible = **RM3,000** (not unlimited!). Period ends 2025.

---

#### 6.2.b Exemption of Annuity Income

**Tax Exemption Details:**

| Aspect | Details | Benefit |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Income Received** | From deferred annuity policy | TAX-FREE ✅ |
| **All Payments** | Every annuity payment exempt | No tax reporting needed |
| **Net Benefit** | Increases effective income | More money in pocket |
| **Advantage** | Makes annuities attractive | Better than taxable investments |

**Example:**
```
Without annuity income exemption:
Annuity payment: RM3,000/month = RM36,000/year
```

**📝 EXAM QUESTION 7** *(Links to Section 6.4.b - Annuity Limitations)*

**Answer**: D (Provide for heirs - FALSE)

**🔑 KEY INSIGHT**: Annuities are for **ANNUITANT'S INCOME**, not for **LEAVING INHERITANCE**!

**ANALYSIS OF EACH**:

**A** ✅ **TRUE** statement:
- Fixed annuities DO suffer from inflation
- RM3,000 today ≠ RM3,000 in 20 years
- This is a KNOWN PROBLEM with fixed annuities

**B** ✅ **TRUE** statement:
- If can't handle investment risk with lump sum
- Annuity = Professional management
- Guaranteed income (especially fixed)
- Good option for risk-averse

**C** ✅ **TRUE** statement:
- Annuity = Conversion of lump sum to income stream
- Regular, predictable payments
- This IS what annuity does!

**D** ❌ **FALSE** statement - This is the WRONG answer:
- Annuities are for **ANNUITANT'S RETIREMENT INCOME**
- **NOT designed** to leave money to heirs
- Pure life annuity: **NOTHING** to heirs
- If you want to leave inheritance: BUY LIFE INSURANCE instead!

**DISTINCTION TO UNDERSTAND**:
```
Want regular income for self? → ANNUITY ✅
Want to leave money to heirs? → LIFE INSURANCE ✅

Annuity = LIVE TOO LONG protection (for you)
Life Insurance = DIE TOO SOON protection (for heirs)
```

**EXCEPTION TO NOTE**:
- Annuity certain: CAN provide for heirs (if die during certain period)
- But this is NOT the PRIMARY purpose
- Main purpose still = Income for annuitant

**KEYWORDS**:
- "Provide for heirs" = **LIFE INSURANCE** function, not annuity
- "FALSE" = Look for incorrect statement
- "Annuitant's heirs" = Wrong product focus

**EXAM TIP**: Don't confuse annuity (income for living) with life insurance (money for heirs)!

### Formula & Calculations


```
Tax Savings = Deferred Annuity Premium (max RM3,000) × Tax Rate

Premium paid: RM5,000
Maximum deductible: RM3,000
Tax rate: 14%
Tax savings: RM3,000 × 14% = RM420/year
```

```
Future Value = Present Value / (1 + inflation)^years

Example:
Today's payment: RM3,000/month
Inflation: 3% per year
In 20 years:
Real value = RM3,000 / (1.03)^20 = RM3,000 / 1.806 = RM1,661
Lost 45% of purchasing power!
```
