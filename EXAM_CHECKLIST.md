**Two Schemes**:
```
EIIS (Employment Injury Insurance):
- Work-related injuries/diseases
- NO age limit
- Commuting accidents included

IPS (Invalidity Pension):
- Non-work-related disabilities
- Must be UNDER 60 at diagnosis
- Death any cause
```

**Contribution Rates** (CRITICAL):
- Employee: **0.5%**
- Employer: **1.75%**
- Total: **2.25%**

**Exempted (SGDF)**:
- **S**elf-employed
- **G**overnment servants
- **D**omestic workers
- **F**oreign workers

**Wages**:
- INCLUDE: Salary, allowances, overtime, **leave payments**
- EXCLUDE: Annual bonus, employer statutory contributions

## ⚠️ TOP 20 EXAM TRAPS

1. **Contract workers** → COVERED by SOCSO (not exempted)
2. **NAMLIA** → Does NOT exist (it's LIAM)
3. **MRTA vs MLTA** → MRTA reduces, MLTA level
4. **Age 60 SOCSO** → IPS stops at 60 (not continue)
5. **Can claim both** → SOCSO + other insurance (not exclusive)
6. **0.5% employee** → Not 0.75% (don't confuse with EPF)
7. **Bonus = wages** → Annual bonus EXCLUDED
8. **Leave payments** → INCLUDED in SOCSO wages
9. **NCD certificate** → Must get from previous insurer
10. **Digital access** → NOT one of 8 basic consumer rights
11. **Banking Security Bond** → NOT construction bond (only TAP)
12. **Employee negligence** → NOT covered in Employer's Liability
13. **CAR during operation** → CAR for installation, Machinery for operation
14. **Guaranteed Takaful profits** → NO guarantees in Islamic finance
15. **Wakalah = profit share** → Wakalah gets FEE only
16. **Two transaction conditions** → ALL THREE required (DID)
17. **Agent acts for client** → Acts for INSURER when filling forms
18. **Fixed annuity no inflation risk** → Has inflation risk
19. **Pure life to heirs** → NOTHING to heirs (fully liquidated)
20. **After vs On/After** → "On or after" is inclusive
